I'm a Ph.D. student at the [Department of Logistics and Maritime Studies](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/lms/), [Faculty of Business](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/fb/), [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), supervised by [Prof. Shining Wu](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/lms/people/academic-staff/shining-wu/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Operations Research, Stochastic Optimization, and LLM Inference Systems**. I work on **OR for LLM** — using stochastic modeling, online decision-making, and optimization to design provably efficient inference systems, with a focus on scheduling, batching, and resource allocation under uncertainty.

You are more than welcome to contact me if you want to know more about me or collaborate with me.
