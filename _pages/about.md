I am currently a Ph.D. student at the [Department of Logistics and Maritime Studies](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/lms/), [Faculty of Business](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/fb/), [The Hong Kong Polytechnic University](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/), advised by [Prof. Shining Wu](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/lms/people/academic-staff/shining-wu/). Prior to that, I earned my Master's degree in Applied Statistics from [Renmin University of China](https://www.ruc.edu.cn/) in 2024, and my Bachelor's degree in Economic Statistics from [Southwestern University of Finance and Economics](https://e.swufe.edu.cn/) in 2022.

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Operations Research, Stochastic Optimization, and LLM Inference Systems**. I work on **OR for LLM** — using stochastic modeling, online decision-making, and optimization to design provably efficient inference systems, with a focus on scheduling, batching, and resource allocation under uncertainty.
